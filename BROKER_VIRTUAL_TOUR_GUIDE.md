# 📸 Broker Guide: Creating Virtual Tours with Your Phone

## What is a Virtual Tour?

A **Virtual Tour** is an interactive 360° viewing experience that lets potential buyers explore your property as if they were standing there in person. They can look around in all directions, zoom in on details, and navigate between different viewpoints.

**Good news:** You don't need expensive 360° cameras! Your regular smartphone is all you need.

---

## 🎯 Why Use Virtual Tours?

-   ✨ **Stand out** - Properties with virtual tours get 87% more views

## 📸 Step-by-Step: Taking Virtual Tour Photos

### Planning Your Shots

**For Land Properties:**

1. **Entrance view** - Show the access road and entry point
2. **Overview shots** - Multiple positions showing the full plot
3. **Boundary views** - Stand at different corners
4. **Special features** - Water sources, trees, slopes, views
5. **Surroundings** - Nearby roads, neighbors, landmarks

**For Houses/Buildings:**

1. **Exterior** - Front, sides, back, and roof (if elevated)
2. **Each room** - Stand in the center, capture 360°
3. **Kitchen & bathrooms** - Important selling points
4. **Views from windows** - Show what you see outside
5. **Special features** - Pools, gardens, garages

### Camera Technique

#### Option 1: Using Google Street View App (Recommended)

1. Download "Street View" app (Android/iPhone)
2. Open app → Tap camera icon → "Camera"
3. Select "360 Photo"
4. Hold phone upright, follow the on-screen dots
5. App auto-stitches into perfect 360° photo
6. Save to your phone

#### Option 2: Using Built-in Panorama Mode

1. Open your phone's camera
2. Switch to "Panorama" or "Pano" mode
3. Stand in one spot, rotate slowly 360°
4. Keep the phone level and steady
5. Complete the full circle
6. Photo saves automatically

#### Option 3: Regular Photos (Manual Method)

1. Stand in one spot
2. Take 8-12 photos in a circle around you:
    - 0° (North), 45°, 90° (East), 135°, 180° (South), 225°, 270° (West), 315°
3. Overlap each photo by 30-40%
4. Keep camera at same height
5. Use a free app like "Microsoft ICE" to stitch together later

### Pro Tips for Great Photos

✅ **DO:**

-   Shoot on a clear, bright day (10am-3pm is best)
-   Keep the phone steady (use tripod or lean on something)
-   Take photos from chest/eye height for natural perspective
-   Clean your camera lens first!
-   Take 4-6 different viewpoints per property
-   Capture the "hero shot" - the most impressive view

❌ **DON'T:**

-   Shoot in harsh noon sun (washed out) or late afternoon (too orange)
-   Include yourself in the photo (check shadows!)
-   Rush - take 2-3 attempts per spot if needed
-   Shoot in the rain or heavy clouds
-   Block important features with your body

## 💾 Uploading to GeoCasa Platform

### Method 1: Through Property Edit Page (Recommended)

1. **Log in** to your broker account
2. Go to **"My Properties"**
3. Click **"Edit"** on the property
4. Scroll to **"Virtual Tour"** section
5. Click **"Upload Virtual Tour Images"**
6. Select your 360° photos (up to 10 images)
7. Optionally add **hotspots** (labels for special features)
8. Toggle **"Enable Virtual Tour"** to ON
9. Click **"Save Changes"**

### Method 2: Through Admin Panel (If you have access)

1. Navigate to **Admin → Properties**
2. Find your property and click **"Edit"**
3. Upload images in **"Virtual Tour Images"** field
4. Enable **"Has Virtual Tour"** checkbox
5. Save

### What Happens Next:

✨ Your property will show a **"🌟 360° Virtual Tour Available"** badge  
🔍 Buyers can filter specifically for properties with virtual tours  
📈 Your property appears higher in search results  
💡 Interactive viewer loads on the property detail page

## 🎨 Adding Interactive Hotspots (Optional)

**Hotspots** are clickable labels that highlight special features in your virtual tour.

### When to Use:

-   Point out **special features** ("Mango tree - 15 years old")
-   Highlight **recent improvements** ("New fence - 2024")
-   Show **boundaries** ("Property line extends to road")
-   Note **utilities** ("Electric connection here")
-   Indicate **views** ("Beach view from this corner")

### How to Add:

1. While editing property, upload your virtual tour images first
2. Click **"Add Hotspot"** for each image
3. **Position** - Click where on the 360° photo the hotspot should appear
4. **Label** - Brief text (e.g., "Water source", "Boundary marker")
5. **Description** (optional) - More details if needed
6. Save

## 📊 Best Practices by Property Type

### 🏞️ Raw Land / Lot

**Minimum shots:** 4-6 viewpoints

-   **Shot 1:** Entrance/access road view
-   **Shot 2:** Center of lot looking north
-   **Shot 3:** Center looking south (opposite direction)
-   **Shot 4:** Corner showing boundaries
-   **Shot 5:** Any special feature (tree, stream, slope)
-   **Shot 6:** View of surroundings/neighborhood

### 🏠 House / Residential

**Minimum shots:** 8-12 viewpoints

-   Exterior: Front, back, side with best features
-   Interior: Living room, kitchen, master bedroom, bathrooms
-   Special: Garden, terrace, garage, views

### 🏢 Commercial Property

**Minimum shots:** 6-10 viewpoints

-   Street view showing location visibility
-   Entrance and facade
-   Main floor space
-   Additional floors/rooms if applicable
-   Parking area
-   Nearby landmarks/foot traffic

### 🏗️ Development/Multi-unit

**Minimum shots:** 10-15 viewpoints

-   Overall site overview (4 corners)
-   Access roads
-   Infrastructure (utilities, drainage)
-   Sample unit interiors (if built)
-   Surrounding area context

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "Photos won't upload"

-   **Check file size:** Each photo should be under 10 MB
-   **Check format:** JPG/JPEG works best
-   **Reduce resolution:** Use phone's "Medium" quality setting
-   **Try one at a time:** Upload individually if batch fails

### "360° viewer not showing"

-   **Verify toggle:** Make sure "Enable Virtual Tour" is ON
-   **Check image count:** Need at least 1 image uploaded
-   **Clear cache:** Refresh the property page (Ctrl+F5)
-   **File path:** Ensure images uploaded to correct property

### "Photos look distorted"

-   **Use panorama mode:** Regular photos need stitching first
-   **Stay level:** Keep phone parallel to ground
-   **Rotate smoothly:** Don't jerk or tilt while shooting
-   **Good lighting:** Avoid extreme bright/dark contrasts

### "Buyer says it won't load"

-   **Internet speed:** Virtual tours need decent connection
-   **Mobile data:** May not load on slow mobile data
-   **Browser:** Works best on Chrome, Firefox, Safari (updated versions)
-   **Try different device:** Desktop usually loads faster than mobile
