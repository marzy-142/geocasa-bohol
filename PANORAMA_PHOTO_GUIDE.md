# 📸 How to Create Panorama Photos for Virtual Tours

## Overview

GeoCasa Bohol now supports **both regular photo galleries and 360° panorama views** for property listings! You don't need expensive specialized cameras - you can create stunning panoramic views using just your smartphone.

## What's Available

### 1. **Photo Gallery** 📷

-   Regular property photos
-   Multiple images showcasing different angles
-   Thumbnail navigation
-   Fullscreen viewing

### 2. **360° Panorama Viewer** 🌐

-   Interactive panoramic images
-   Drag to explore
-   Scroll to zoom
-   Immersive property experience

## How to Take Panorama Photos with Your Smartphone

### For iPhone Users:

1. Open the **Camera** app
2. Swipe to **"PANO"** mode
3. Tap the shutter button
4. Slowly pan your phone from left to right (follow the arrow)
5. Keep the arrow centered on the line
6. Tap the shutter button when finished

### For Android Users:

1. Open **Google Camera** or your default camera app
2. Select **"Panorama"** or **"Photo Sphere"** mode
3. Tap the shutter button
4. Slowly move your phone following the on-screen guide
5. Tap the shutter button when finished

### For Best Results:

✅ **DO:**

-   Stand in one spot and rotate your body
-   Keep the phone vertical and level
-   Move slowly and steadily
-   Take photos in good lighting (avoid harsh midday sun)
-   Choose a central position in the room/area
-   Remove clutter from the scene

❌ **DON'T:**

-   Walk while taking the panorama
-   Move too fast
-   Tilt the phone up or down
-   Include moving objects (people, pets, cars)
-   Take panoramas in very dim lighting

## Uploading to GeoCasa Bohol

### When Creating/Editing a Property:

1. **Upload Regular Photos:**

    - Go to the "Property Images" section
    - Upload multiple photos showing different views
    - These will appear in the photo gallery tab

2. **Upload Panorama Photos:**
    - Check the "Enable Virtual Tour" checkbox
    - Go to the "Virtual Tour Images" section
    - Upload your panorama photo(s)
    - These will appear in the 360° Panorama tab

## Tips for Great Property Panoramas

### Interior Shots:

-   Stand in the center of the room
-   Make sure the room is clean and well-lit
-   Open curtains for natural light
-   Turn on all lights
-   Remove personal items

### Exterior Shots:

-   Stand at a focal point of the property
-   Capture the full view of the building/lot
-   Include surrounding landscape
-   Shoot during "golden hour" (early morning or late afternoon)
-   Show access roads and nearby landmarks

### For Beach/Agricultural Properties:

-   Capture the full breadth of the property
-   Show the beachfront or farm area completely
-   Include views of water, mountains, or landscape
-   Stand where potential buyers would want to stand

## Technical Requirements

### File Format:

-   JPEG or JPG format
-   Minimum recommended resolution: 2048 x 1024 pixels
-   Maximum file size: 10MB per image

### Best Panorama Settings:

-   **Aspect Ratio:** 2:1 (equirectangular format)
-   **Resolution:** Higher is better (most phones capture at 4000+ pixels wide)
-   **Quality:** Maximum quality setting on your camera app

## Using the Feature on GeoCasa Bohol

### For Property Viewers:

1. Visit any property detail page
2. If the property has both photos and panoramas, you'll see **two tabs**:
    - **Photo Gallery** - Browse regular property photos
    - **360° Panorama** - Explore the interactive panoramic view
3. Click/tap to switch between tabs
4. In panorama mode:
    - Drag with mouse/finger to look around
    - Scroll/pinch to zoom in/out
    - Click fullscreen for immersive experience

### For Brokers:

1. Regular photos go to "Property Images" section
2. Panorama photos go to "Virtual Tour Images" section
3. Check "Enable Virtual Tour" to activate panorama viewer
4. Both will display in a beautiful tabbed interface

## Sample Workflow

### Property Listing with Full Media:

1. Take 5-8 regular photos of the property from different angles
2. Take 1-3 panorama photos of key areas:
    - Main living area (for residential)
    - Full lot view (for land)
    - Beachfront view (for beach properties)
3. Upload regular photos to "Property Images"
4. Enable Virtual Tour and upload panoramas to "Virtual Tour Images"
5. Save the property
6. Viewers will see both gallery and panorama tabs!
